**Normal speed** is the traditional Tai Chi practice tempo — slow, meditative, and deliberate. The generous gaps between posture cues leave room to sink into each movement with full attention to weight shifts, alignment, and internal energy flow. If your movement finishes before the next cue arrives, use the remaining time not to freeze, but to settle deeply into the stance, breathe naturally, and maintain internal awareness. The silence between calls is not dead air; it is an invitation to sink your energy into the Dantian and sustain the continuous flow before the next movement begins.

**Medium Tempo flow** (pigua) is a moderately paced Tai Chi practice tempo — faster than slow meditative form work, but not full application speed. Each posture name is called out as an audio cue with a timed gap before the next, so the practitioner can follow along without memorizing the sequence. This pacing is ideal for learning the posture order and building smooth transitions and flow between movements
