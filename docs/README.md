# Yang 85 form

> **Pigua speed** (披挂速度) is a moderately-paced taichi practice tempo — faster
> than slow meditative form work, but not combat speed. Each posture name is
> called out as an audio cue with a timed gap before the next, so the
> practitioner can follow along without memorising the sequence. This pacing
> is ideal for:
> - learning the posture order,
> - building smooth transitions and flow between movements,
> - bridging slow form practice with application-speed work.
>
> The mp3 files below provide called cues at pigua speed for the full form
> and its parts. Load one, follow the voice, and focus on your movement.
